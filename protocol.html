01—03 · DIAGNOSE & PLAN

Understand before planting.

  1. 01

    Map the land with the Terra Box

    A Terra agronomist visits the plot, registers its boundaries and records its history, water access and the farmer’s objectives. Georeferenced measurements from the Box’s electrical-conductivity (EC) sensor help identify contrasting soil zones and choose representative sampling points.

    DELIVERABLEA field profile, an EC zoning map and a sampling plan.
    Terra field agronomist + Terra Box
  2. 02

    Understand the soil in Terra Lab

    Samples from each zone are identified, logged and sent to Terra Laboratoire Agronomique. Laboratory analysis establishes the nutrient baseline: nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, plus pH, organic matter and other indicators selected for the crop and soil. Methods, units and sampling dates remain attached to the results.

    DELIVERABLEA laboratory report linked to each sampled soil zone.
    Terra Laboratoire Agronomique
  3. 03

    Build the agronomic plan

    Terra’s agronomist combines the map, laboratory results, local climate and the farmer’s resources. Together they select suitable crops and varieties, prepare a seasonal calendar, and define soil preparation, nutrition, water management, crop protection and realistic production objectives.

    DELIVERABLEA practical crop calendar, input budget and farm action plan.
    Terra agronomist + farmer

04—06 · EQUIP & SUPPORT

Connect. Observe. Act.

  1. 04

    Install and connect the Terra Box

    Boxes are placed at representative locations chosen from the diagnosis. The team installs the soil and crop sensors, solar panel and battery, checks readings and radio coverage, and links each device to the right field. The farmer learns how to receive advice and contact the Terra agronomist.

    DELIVERABLEA commissioned installation and a farmer ready to use the service.
    Terra installation team + agronomist
  2. 05

    Monitor continuously and interpret

    The Box follows soil moisture, temperature, pH and EC, complemented by crop and atmospheric sensors. Terra combines these time-stamped observations with the lab baseline, crop stage, weather context and farm history. Analysis highlights unusual changes and prepares advice for agronomic review.

    DELIVERABLEA continuous field record, prioritized alerts and draft recommendations.
    Terra sensors + data platform + AI
  3. 06

    Act with the agronomist

    Terra’s agronomist checks the evidence, adjusts the recommendation to local conditions and validates the action. Clear SMS advice reaches the farmer’s basic phone. Field visits, crop scouting and farmer feedback help confirm what is happening and record what was actually done.

    DELIVERABLEValidated advice and a dated intervention log.
    Terra agronomist + farmer

07—10 · MEASURE & IMPROVE

Make every season count.

  1. 07

    Prepare and record the harvest

    The farmer and agronomist review crop maturity, harvest timing, labor, handling and storage. Terra keeps the harvest linked to the correct field, crop and season so results can be understood alongside the growing conditions and interventions.

    DELIVERABLEAn identified harvest and a record of quality and losses.
    Farmer with Terra agronomic support
  2. 08

    Measure what the farm produced

    Record harvested weight and harvested area to calculate yield. Follow the water, inputs, labor and costs actually recorded, and document the measurement method. Comparisons use a clear baseline and account for crop, season and weather differences.

    DELIVERABLEA measured seasonal scorecard, including yield in kg/ha.
    Terra agronomist + farmer
  3. 09

    Explain the results

    The agronomist reviews results against the plan, the soil diagnosis, sensor records and actual practices. Terra helps identify possible constraints and useful patterns. Field observations and, where needed, new laboratory samples guide which changes to test next.

    DELIVERABLEA seasonal review with documented lessons and priorities.
    Terra agronomist + analytical tools
  4. 10

    Build the farm’s memory

    Maps, laboratory reports, readings, advice, interventions and harvest results stay connected to the farm. The next season starts from this knowledge. Terra’s agronomist updates the plan; models can improve as sufficient, comparable field evidence is collected and validated.

    DELIVERABLEAn improved plan for season two, then a repeatable learning cycle.
    Terra farm record + agronomist

TERRA LAB

Terra Laboratoire
Agronomique.

Our laboratory connects the chemistry of the soil to the decisions made in the field.

Terra’s own laboratory is part of the protocol, working with our field agronomists. It provides the analytical baseline used to understand fertility, interpret sensor observations and prepare a crop nutrition plan.

EC tells us where to investigate.
The Terra Box maps differences in electrical conductivity. Moisture, texture, temperature and salts affect this signal; an EC zone is a guide for investigation, not a direct NPK measurement. EC reference ↗
The laboratory establishes the nutrient reference.
N, P and K are measured in prepared samples using the selected laboratory methods. Reports distinguish the indicators measured, their units and their interpretation; total nutrients and plant-available nutrients are different measurements.
The agronomist brings the evidence together.
Laboratory findings, crop needs, field observations and sensor trends inform the plan. Unexpected changes can trigger a visit, a sensor check or a new sample.

OUR OWN FIELD AGRONOMISTS

A person beside the farmer.
A plan the farmer can follow.

Terra’s agents support empty land and active farms: diagnosis, sampling, planning, farmer training, scheduled visits and review of alerts. The relationship continues after the Box is installed.

01

Crop choice & calendar

Match varieties, planting windows, rotations and harvest planning to the site and the farmer’s objectives.

02

Soil & fertility

Plan soil preparation, organic amendments and nutrient inputs from laboratory evidence and crop requirements.

03

Water & field management

Define irrigation checks, drainage, soil cover and maintenance according to available water and equipment.

04

Crop health & good practices

Organize scouting, prevention, hygiene and locally appropriate crop protection with the agronomist.

05

Resources & responsibilities

Plan labor, tools, input purchasing and the budget. Give each task an owner and a date.

06

Follow-up & success criteria

Schedule agronomist visits, sensor checks and lab reviews; agree how yield, quality and costs will be measured.

AI prepares and prioritizes. The agronomist checks, adapts and validates. The farmer acts and shares feedback. This human loop is part of the protocol.

DESIGNED FOR THE REALITIES OF THE DRC

The Terra Box.
Intelligence rooted in the field.

Solar-powered. No internet or SIM card in each field Box. Useful advice on a basic phone.

Installed directly in the field, the autonomous Box combines sensors, low-power electronics, a solar panel and a rechargeable battery. Its purpose is to keep observing real growing conditions, day and night, with autonomy verified for the local installation.

Soil
Moisture · temperature · pH · electrical conductivity (EC).
Crop & atmosphere
Leaf wetness and temperature; air temperature, humidity and pressure, according to the installed sensors.
Nutrient baseline
Laboratory-measured N, P and K from Terra Lab complement the continuous sensor record.

INSIDE THE TERRA BOX

Three connections.
One intelligent field node.

RAK3112ESP32-S3 + Semtech SX1262

The module at the heart of the Terra Box combines local computing with three complementary wireless links.

01

LoRa / LoRaWAN

Long-range field communication. Compact sensor readings travel from each Box to a shared gateway, without a SIM card in the Box.

02

Wi-Fi

Local configuration, maintenance and network access where Wi-Fi is available, according to the installed Terra firmware.

03

Bluetooth Low Energy

A short-range link for nearby devices and local setup workflows supported by the Terra firmware. BLE complements the farm’s LoRa link.

EDGE AI · ESP32-S3

Process information close to the soil.

The ESP32-S3 provides local processing for sensor acquisition, data preparation, configured rules and lightweight AI models. Its vector instructions support neural-network and signal-processing workloads. Terra combines this embedded layer with cloud analysis, farm history and the agronomist’s judgement.

How local follow-up becomes national infrastructure.

Terra links each farm to its agronomist, laboratory results and devices. As deployment expands, this shared structure can coordinate visits, input needs, harvest planning and support across cooperatives and regions. Role-based access allows institutions, researchers and service providers to use appropriate information, with permission for identifiable farm data.
